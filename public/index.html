About masterNordak

Founding Artist

Nic Nurge

Artist & Developer

I’m a cybersecurity leader by day, creative technology student by night, and apparently a founding artist now because the internet lets us invent titles and put them on cards. My work usually starts somewhere between code, systems, pixels, and “what happens if I break this in an interesting way?” I’m drawn to projects that mix structure with weirdness, especially when I can take something technical and turn it into something people might actually want to look at.

A lot of my creative work lives in the space between digital art, Web3, generative systems, and interactive design. I like the idea of using technology as more than just a productivity machine slowly stealing everyone’s will to live. For me, creative tech is about building small worlds, visual systems, and experiences that feel personal, even when they are made with code, algorithms, or tools that sound like they were named by a committee of exhausted robots.

This site is a collection of experiments, class projects, visual artifacts, and whatever else survives the process without catching fire. Some pieces are polished, some are prototypes, and some are probably evidence that I clicked “run” one too many times and decided to call the result art. Either way, the goal is to keep building, learning, and creating work that blends technical skill with a little personality, because “technically functional” is not exactly a personality trait.
